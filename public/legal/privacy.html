Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 21, 2026

1. What We Collect

Account data: When you sign in with Google OAuth, we receive your name and email address. When you sign in with email verification, we collect your email address.

Receipt data: After OCR processing, we store structured text extracted from your receipts: vendor name, amount, currency, date, invoice number, category, tax amount, and the raw OCR text. We do not store the original receipt files (photos, PDFs).

Usage data: We track scan counts for credit billing. We do not use analytics scripts, tracking cookies, or third-party trackers.

2. What We Don't Collect

We do not store original receipt images or PDFs after processing. Files are read into memory, OCR'd, and discarded. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any third-party tracking services. We do not sell, share, or rent your data to anyone.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your receipt data is used solely to provide the Service: displaying your invoices, enabling search and filtering, and generating CSV exports. Your email is used for account verification, passwordless login, and service announcements.

4. Data Storage

All data is stored in a SQLite database on a Hetzner VPS in Germany, protected by Cloudflare Tunnel (TLS encryption in transit). API keys are SHA-256 hashed before storage. Session tokens are random 64-character hex strings.

5. Third-Party Services

We rely on the following third-party services:

  • Google OAuth — for sign-in. Google receives your name and email per their privacy policy.
  • SendGrid — for sending email verification codes. Your email address is transmitted to SendGrid for delivery.
  • Stripe — for payment processing. When you purchase credits, you interact with Stripe's checkout page. We do not handle or store your payment card details. Stripe's privacy policy applies to the payment transaction.
  • Tesseract.js — OCR runs entirely on our server. No data leaves our VPS during OCR processing.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Receipt data is retained until you delete it or delete your account. When you delete an invoice, it moves to trash and is permanently deleted after 30 days. When you request account deletion, your account is scheduled for deletion after 30 days. During this grace period, you can cancel the deletion by logging in. After 30 days, all your data is permanently removed.

7. Cookies

We use one session cookie (laibox_session) to keep you logged in. It is HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict, and Secure (in production). It expires after 7 days of inactivity. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

8. Your Rights

You may access, correct, export, or delete your data at any time through the app. For data export, use the CSV export feature. For full data deletion, use the account deletion option in settings. Contact support@tsuibian.com for assistance.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be communicated via email. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? support@tsuibian.com
